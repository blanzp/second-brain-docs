# Offline reading

On a plane, in the mountains, in a basement: your notes come with you.

## How it works

- **Every note you open** is saved on your device automatically.
- **Keep a whole folder offline:** tap ☁︎ on the folder. Every note in it and its subfolders is downloaded, with images and diagrams. Kept folders refresh whenever the app opens online.
- **Offline, it just works:**
    - saved notes open with an *"Offline · saved copy from…"* line;
    - search runs over your saved notes;
    - any folder can be browsed, showing what's saved;
    - diagrams still show.
- On a weak or stuck connection, the saved copy appears after a few seconds instead of an endless spinner.

## See what's on your device

The **Offline** tab lists the folders you keep offline, when they were last refreshed (with **Refresh now**), and every saved note grouped by folder. In lists, saved notes carry a small cloud mark.

## Good to know

- Editing waits for a connection; your saved copies are read-only offline.
- **Signing out deletes everything saved** on that device.
- On iPhone, open the app online before you travel: iOS can clear a site's saved data if it isn't used for a few weeks.
