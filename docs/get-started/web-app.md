# 4. Open the web app

Open **[brain.mdcrypt.dev/app](https://brain.mdcrypt.dev/app/)** and sign in with GitHub. That's it: your vault is ready to search, read and edit from any browser.

## Add it to your home screen

Use it like a native app, full screen with its own icon:

=== "iPhone"
    In **Safari**: tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.

=== "Android"
    In **Chrome**: tap **⋮** → **Install app**.

=== "Computer"
    In Chrome or Edge: click the **install** icon in the address bar. Or just bookmark it.

## A quick tour

- **Home:** search everything (words, `#tags`, or both) and see what changed recently and who changed it.
- **Folders:** browse your vault. Tap ☁︎ on a folder to keep it **offline**.
- **New:** create a note in any folder, or in a new subfolder.
- **Tasks:** every todo from every note, with due dates, in collapsible sections.
- **Offline:** everything saved on this device.
- **⚙ Settings:** your app title, where new notes go, your time zone and more.

Full details: [The web app](../features/web-app.md).

!!! tip "Pull to refresh"
    Pull down at the top of any screen to reload it. Changes made by your agents show up straight away.
