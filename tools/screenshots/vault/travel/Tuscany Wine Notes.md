# Tuscany Wine Notes

## Brunello

Sangiovese from Montalcino, aged at least five years. Worth the splurge at a winery, not a restaurant.

## Chianti Classico

Look for the black rooster on the neck.

## Vernaccia

The white of San Gimignano: crisp, a little bitter almond.
